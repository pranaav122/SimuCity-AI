# Empirical Observations & Research Notes

## 1. Primary Empirical Findings

Across initial controlled baseline trials ($N=16$ agents, $T=288$ ticks / 3 days):

1. **Spontaneous Clustering Under Academic Pressure**:
   - When academic stress is elevated by upcoming exams, agents with complementary personality traits (high ambition paired with high cooperation) self-organize into stable study clusters at the Main Library and Dining Commons.
2. **Resource Reallocation in Price Shocks**:
   - Following a 25% price increase in cafeteria meals, agents with higher initial liquidity demonstrated emergent altruistic behavior by extending peer loans via `HELP_AGENT` to insolvent friends, preventing starvation and extreme stress spikes.
3. **Information Cascade Dynamics**:
   - High-trust agents acted as pivotal routing hubs in rumor propagation. When official announcements were broadcast, transmission speed through the social graph exhibited classic sigmoidal diffusion curves.
